    Computer Project Management

    Coursework for the Computer Project Management course at Wrocław University of Science and Technology, 2026. One fictional IT project — the Export/Import Management System — planned in microsoft-project and matured across five lab stages, plus a seminar deck on the non-technical side of the discipline. 23 files: ~21 KB of prose and 588 KB of opaque binaries. No code, no build, no manifest.

    Status: coursework, finished and frozen at last commit 2026-07-10. "All finished and frozen" (interview, 2026-07-28). Nothing here is maintained.

    #The five stages

    Each lab folder holds the same plan one technique later — numbered-stages-in-directory-names applied to a document repo rather than a code one.

    Lab Adds Tasks Duration Finish
    lab1_WBS_Practiceoutline mechanics, FS links — a throwaway sandbox2978 d15 Jul 2026

    lab5-6_Baseline_and_Trackinga [[schedule-baseline-and-variancebaseline]], 7 control-point milestones, tracked actuals + progress-report.pdf45134 d8 Sep 2026

    The plan decomposes into 3 phases / 9 task groups / 26 working tasks / 7 zero-day milestones, runs 2 Mar → 8 Sep 2026, and ends at 190 960 zł, entirely labour — nine hourly-rate work resources against equipment, licence and material resources that carry no booked cost.

    #The interesting part is the slip, not the plan

    Between lab 2 and lab 5–6 the finish date moves out twice, for two different reasons, and both are written down. Leveling costs 11 days; then tracking costs another 11.

    "The extra 11 days come from resolving over-allocation: once a resource can only do one"

    Computer Project Management/lab3-4_Resource_Assignment/README.md, 2026-07-10

    The second slip is a genuine planning decision with a rejected alternative: sequence-integration-after-unit-testing. Its knock-on runs down the critical path into the finalization phase.

    #The seminar is the other half

    seminar/it-pm-environments.pptx (15 slides, Topic #12) covers what the labs cannot schedule — PESTLE, EEFs vs OPAs, the socio-technical model, PMBOK vs PRINCE2, toxic team cultures, Brooks' Law, the 4 Ps. Recorded as project-environment-factors. The repo itself states the split:

    "The labs are the hard side of project management — the WBS, the critical path, the budget. This seminar is the soft side"

    Computer Project Management/seminar/README.md, 2026-07-10

    Brooks' Law (slide 11) is the seminar arguing against exactly the recovery action the labs did not take — adding a second Backend Developer to fix the over-allocation.

    #Findings

    • 11 commits in 48 minutes on 2026-07-10, zero fixes, zero merges. A textbook import-finished-work-into-git dump; the last two (e9eb6e2 MIT license, 4af85fb license badges) land 43 minutes later and are pure presentation-polish-tail-commits, byte-similar to the identical pair in physics-exam-prep (15:34:23) and privacy-policy-template (15:10:41). Per interview these dates are consolidation, not release — see import-finished-work-into-git.
    • documentation-drift, one instance. lab2_Task_Dependencies/README.md says tasks are "linked with all four relationship types rather than a single chain" and then tabulates three — FS, SS, FF. Start-to-Finish appears nowhere in the repo. Whether SF was tried and removed or never applied is not recorded. confidence: high on the drift, low on the cause.
    • Binaries declared, but no Git LFS. .gitattributes is four lines of binary markers: > "# Treat the binary coursework artifacts as binary — no line-ending" > — Computer Project Management/.gitattributes, 2026-07-10

    588 KB of .mpp/.pptx/.pdf/.png committed raw. Same posture as optimization-methods, which commits an 11 MB PDF without LFS.

    • The READMEs are the deliverable. Every lab folder is a reader-facing tour with tables, before/after comparisons and figures — readme-as-guided-tour. This is the only repo in the group where the README carries more substance than the artefact it describes, because the artefact is unreadable without Microsoft Project.
    • Variance is reported as raw deltas, not indices. +11 d, +248 h, +14 880 zł. There is no risk register, no PERT estimation and no earned-value CPI/SPI anywhere in the readable files. See schedule-baseline-and-variance.
