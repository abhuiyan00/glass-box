    Report Cites Benchmarks Rather Than Reproducing

    Decision. Scope research-methodologies-open-set-recognition as a written synthesis. Take the OpenGAN benchmark table from the source paper, label it as second-hand in the same view as the numbers, and run no experiments.

    Date. Committed 2026-07-11, in the commit that wrote the main report.

    #Why

    The report states the boundary directly beneath the results table, where a reader meets the numbers rather than in a footnote:

    "These are literature results reported by the OpenGAN authors, reproduced here to explain the"

    Research Methodologies/README.md, 2026-07-11

    "method — not experiments run for this coursework."

    Research Methodologies/README.md, 2026-07-11

    The NOTICE ledger says it a second time and the licence footer a third — see provenance-notice-file. Even the chart is marked "redrawn", not reproduced: the figure is the authors' own drawing of someone else's data, and the distinction is kept.

    #Alternatives rejected

    Option Consequence
    Reproduce OpenGAN on MNIST / CIFAR-10a training pipeline, a frozen backbone, GPU time, and the model-selection step the paper says is the difference between state-of-the-art and random — far outside a written coursework deliverable
    Present the numbers unattributedthe failure mode this decision exists to prevent: a reader takes cited results for the authors' own
    Drop the benchmark tableremoves the one quantitative claim the argument rests on; the report would assert OpenGAN wins with no figure at all
    Reproduce a cheaper baseline (softmax / OpenMax) for comparisonwould have given one independently-run number to anchor the cited ones — not taken, and not discussed

    #What the decision costs

    Nothing in the study independently checks anything it reports. The entire results section — MNIST 99.9, SVHN 98.8, CIFAR-10 97.3, TinyImageNet 90.7 — rests on one source: Kong & Ramanan's Table

    1. Contrast convergent-evidence, where a conclusion is only accepted once independent lines

    agree. Here the claim is single-source and labelled as such, which is the honest version of a weak evidence base rather than a fix for it. Recorded as the central limitation of literature-review-synthesis as practised in this repo.

    The cost lands hardest on the one place the study is arguing something: open-set-recognition also quotes the Vaze et al. finding that a good closed-set classifier is already a good open-set detector, which implies the softmax and OpenMax baselines in that table could themselves be lifted. With no run of its own, the study cannot tell whether OpenGAN's margin survives a stronger baseline. Both claims are printed; neither is resolved.
