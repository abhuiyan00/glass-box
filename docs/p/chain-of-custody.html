    Chain of Custody

    In forensic work, a claim is only as good as the trail from the claim back to the artefact it came from. Modelled in data, that means every assertion must resolve to a document row, and every document row must carry enough to identify the original page.

    #How it is built

    In labour-fraud-analysis, the document table carries a source path, a content hash, a duplicate count and a document class. Three junction tables — document↔person, document↔company, document↔worksite — record which parties each document touches. Nothing in the narrative is supposed to exist without a row behind it.

    "no single date or number here is load-bearing without the underlying page."

    labour-fraud-analysis-a-forensic-data-investigation/docs/investigation.md, 2026-07-11

    That sentence is the whole discipline compressed. It says two things at once: the numbers are evidence, and no individual number is the evidence — which is why the argument is built as convergent-evidence rather than as a single decisive figure.

    #Three mechanisms worth separating

    • Content hashing for deduplication. The corpus folds ~2,456 originals down to 512 distinct records by content hash. This is an ingestion step, not an analytic finding — a scanned page filed three times is one document. Do not read it as duplicate-record fraud detection; no such analytic exists here.
    • Provenance junctions. Because the document↔party links are tables rather than prose, "what does this claim rest on" is a query. This is where third-normal-form pays for itself.
    • Tiered reliability. Claims are graded up front: official documents, single-source testimony, and inference. The tier travels with the claim, so a reader can discount without re-deriving.

    #Data-quality errors are kept, not cleaned

    The corpus contains extraction errors — wrong dates from OCR — and they are preserved deliberately in both the private source and the synthetic derivative. Two reasons, both real: cleaning a value is an unrecorded edit to evidence, and a dataset without the original's defects does not exercise the same cleaning problems. See structure-preserving-anonymisation and provenance-and-limits-stated-inline.

    #The limit of custody through an anonymiser

    Once the corpus passes through the pipeline, the chain leads back to a synthetic document row, and the link to the real page exists only in the git-ignored mapping. The published artefact is therefore internally auditable and externally unverifiable — a deliberate trade, and one more reason every page for this repo stays publish: false. See disclosure-gate and synthesise-rather-than-redact.
