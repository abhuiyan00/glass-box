    Superseded-in-Place Documentation

    When a decision is reversed, the document is not rewritten. A dated SUPERSEDED banner is stacked on top of the original text, and the original stays underneath, readable.

    In stickfps the clearest case is the art direction. Strict film-noir grayscale was a mandatory hard constraint in three separate documents — DESIGN_PROMPT.md §3, docs/DESIGN-BRIEF.md §2 and docs/SRS.md FR-9.3 — and was retired for full colour on 2026-07-24. All three still carry the mandate, banner-stamped rather than deleted. The SRS's retired transport sections are handled the same way: a prose note in constraint C2 rather than an edit to §1.2, §4.3, §4.4, §6.5 or the diagrams.

    #What it buys

    An auditable history in the working tree. A reader can see what was once required and when it stopped being required, which a clean rewrite destroys. It is the documentation form of the same instinct as keep-the-superseded-version-in-tree, and it is why the reversal in websocket-tcp-over-geckos-udp and render-over-fly-io can be reconstructed at all.

    It also lowers the cost of reversing a decision. Rewriting three documents to change the art direction is a task; adding three banners is not. Cheap reversals are worth something in themselves.

    #What it costs

    Internal contradiction, permanently. The document is now two documents interleaved, and a reader who enters below the banner gets the retired answer with no warning. docs/DESIGN-BRIEF.md §7 still reasons from a premise its own banner retired a hundred lines earlier:

    "since play is grayscale"

    browser_game_stickfps/docs/DESIGN-BRIEF.md, 2026-07-25

    Consumers downstream of the prose do not see the banner at all. TASKS.md still directs the deployment at the retired host:

    "Backend to Fly.io (flyctl launch, PORT=9090, UDP enabled)"

    browser_game_stickfps/TASKS.md, 2026-07-25

    #The line between this and drift

    They are easy to confuse and the difference is whether the reversal was marked. A banner over stale text is superseded-in-place: the document knows. EXPOSE 10000/udp in the Dockerfile and the four .tooling files still naming Geckos.io have no banner and no note — nobody knows, and the loop that reads them treats them as current. That is documentation-drift, and it is the failure this pattern is a partial defence against, not a form of it.

    The practice is only sound if the banner sits where the reader enters. Applied to a long document read in sections, it degrades into drift with a disclaimer at the top.
