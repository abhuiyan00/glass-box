    Convergent Evidence

    An argument built from several independently weak signals that point the same way, rather than from one decisive test. Each signal on its own has an innocent explanation; the claim is that the conjunction does not. labour-fraud-analysis states the frame explicitly — "not a smoking gun, a convergence", and "No single one proves intent".

    #The signals

    Signal Computation Weakness taken alone
    Bulk sponsorshipgroup permits by (day, employer); flag days above a thresholda genuine large employer hires in batches
    Shared identifiersvalue-count concentration on contact fieldsshared inboxes are common and legal
    Wage uniformitymodal spike in a 30-bin histogram of contract valuesa standard rate produces the same spike
    Entity successionpermits migrating predecessor→successor across a yearordinary corporate restructuring
    Network bridgingpeople adjacent to two or more companies in an entity graphpeople change jobs

    Each is reproduced identically in the narrative, the dashboard, the notebook and the tests — the reproducible-analysis property that keeps the four surfaces from disagreeing.

    #What the method is, precisely

    Threshold flags, group-bys, value-count concentration, and a graph — then an argument. Stating that plainly matters more than it might seem, because a reader who sees "forensic data analysis" will assume a standard toolkit that is not present here:

    • no Benford's law
    • no z-score, IQR, or any statistical outlier detection
    • no time-series anomaly model
    • no significance testing of any kind
    • no coefficient of variation or entropy behind the "uniformity" read — it is a visual modal spike
    • no centrality measure; graph degree is used only for node sizing, and "bridging" is a neighbour count
    • no ghost-employee test as such — the outsized payroll line and the implausible-age employees are narrative observations, not computed
    • no duplicate-record detection as an analytic; the content-hash folding is an ingestion step, see chain-of-custody

    None of that is a defect of the argument, which never claims statistical inference. It is a correction to what a reader would otherwise assume.

    #Convergence needs independence, and that is the soft spot

    The signals are only mutually reinforcing if they are independent. Several are computed from the same permit table, so a single upstream irregularity could produce more than one of them. Nothing in the repo argues for independence. Compare dual-implementation-as-cross-check, where independence is the entire design goal.

    #Gap

    • TODO: permit-number contiguity is described as the strongest single element of the bulk-sponsorship case and appears as a table of ranges in the narrative — but it is asserted by eye and never computed. The notebook prints a sorted list of serials for the largest batch; nothing measures gaps. The anonymiser is documented as preserving contiguity via a constant offset, which would keep it true, but no check confirms either. confidence: low on contiguity as a finding.
    • TODO: the bulk-filing threshold is hard-coded identically in three places with no derivation recorded — legal threshold, empirical break, or round number is unknown. confidence: low on the threshold's justification. See labour-fraud-analysis.
