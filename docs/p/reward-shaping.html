    Reward Shaping

    The task in it-applications-in-business-and-commerce is "drive a fast, clean lap". That is a sparse, delayed signal — you learn nothing until the lap ends, and a policy that crashes at turn one never sees it. Reward shaping replaces it with a dense per-step surrogate: a reward the agent can follow gradient on at every timestep.

    The risk is the whole subject. A surrogate reward is a hypothesis about what fast driving is made of, and the agent will optimise the surrogate, not the intent. Shaping terms that reward speed produce a car that goes fast into a wall; terms that reward staying centred produce a car that never takes a racing line.

    #Four formulations, one algorithm

    Six of the eight experiment branches are reward experiments — the algorithm is held constant while the reward is varied. That is the structure of branch-per-experiment, and it is what makes the branch reports readable as an experiment log rather than a changelog. The distinct formulations documented with pseudocode across docs/branch-reports/:

    Formulation The hypothesis
    Straightness / progressreward longitudinal progress along the track axis, penalise lateral drift and heading error
    Danger — time-to-edgereward is a function of how long until you leave the track at the current heading and speed, so the penalty arrives before the crash rather than at it
    RPM bandreward holding the engine in its torque band — superseded by measurement in data-driven-gearing-over-hand-tuned-rpm-bands
    Jerkpenalise the derivative of acceleration to suppress the oscillation the speed rewards produced

    The stable tree — the one that shipped — uses the danger formulation, with off_track_penalty = 10000.0 dominating everything else, plus target_speed = 360, corner_speed = 120 and brake_enable_speed = 80.0 as the speed-shaping terms. The intent recorded for it is learning when to brake without hand-coded braking zones.

    Time-to-edge is the interesting one: it converts a discrete terminal event into a continuous quantity the policy can see coming, which is exactly what shaping is supposed to do.
