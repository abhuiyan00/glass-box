    Literature Review Synthesis

    Narrative synthesis of an existing literature into one argued account, as opposed to producing new data. It is a research method in its own right, with its own design decisions and its own failure modes — and it is the method research-methodologies-open-set-recognition actually used, despite the course name suggesting the repo would be about methods rather than an instance of one.

    #The design, as executed

    A funnel with a widening at the end:

    1. Frame the problem — what closed-set classification assumes and where the assumption breaks.
    2. Map the field — a three-family taxonomy (traditional / statistical / deep) so every cited method has a place to sit.
    3. Deep-dive one family — the generative branch, developed from GAN fundamentals upward.
    4. Spotlight one method — OpenGAN, taken to its design details.
    5. Compare on benchmarks — a results table lifted from the spotlight paper.

    Steps 1–2 are breadth, 3–4 are depth, 5 is the payoff. Nine references support it; two seminar decks were the raw material, reworked into one document.

    #What replaces an experiment

    There is no dataset, no code, no sampling frame and no hypothesis test anywhere in the repo. The quality-control mechanism is therefore not reproducibility but a stated provenance boundary — what is the authors' own, and what is not, marked in three separate places: a NOTICE ledger (provenance-notice-file), an inline note under the results table, and the licence footer.

    "It summarises and cites third-party scholarly work, which remains the property"

    Research Methodologies/NOTICE, 2026-07-11

    That substitution is the scoping decision recorded as report-cites-benchmarks-rather-than-reproducing. It is honest, and it is also the method's weakest point: nothing in the study independently checks anything it reports.

    #The failure modes, visible in this instance

    A synthesis is only as strong as its selection and attribution discipline, and this one has three recorded soft spots — all noted rather than smoothed over:

    • Unjustified selection. OpenGAN is the spotlight; no selection criterion is recorded, though four rival methods are cited. A synthesis that cannot say why it zoomed in where it did has substituted a preference for a method.
    • Name-dropping without engagement. docs/01 §4.4 lists evidential deep learning, Bayesian neural networks, contrastive learning and vision transformers in a single bullet run, with no definitions and no per-item citations. Breadth claimed, not demonstrated.
    • A dangling reference. Reference 9 appears in two bibliographies and is tied to no claim in either document.

    Contrast convergent-evidence, where a conclusion is only accepted once independent lines agree: this study's benchmark claims rest on one source table, self-declared as such. Single-source by design and labelled — but single-source.
