    Systems Analysis and Decision Support

    Least squares and ridge regression, κ-NN, Bernoulli naive Bayes, and logistic regression fitted by both gradient ascent and IRLS — in pure Python with no NumPy. 75 tests. A clean-room-rebuild-from-specification of the sixth-semester SADiSM laboratories.

    #Why no NumPy

    NumPy is the sensible engineering choice and is deliberately absent. Every question this subject is worth studying for is a question about the solve step or the arithmetic around it, and delegating those answers none of them: why ridge is stable where ordinary least squares is not, what ill-conditioning costs in digits, why a naive sigmoid raises OverflowError, why a product of probabilities has to become a sum of logs.

    This is zero-dependency-no-build-step chosen for pedagogy rather than for deployment — the dependency would not have been a burden, it would have been an answer skipped.

    #Four measurements

    Regularisation, in digits. λI shifts every eigenvalue of the Gram matrix up by exactly λ. Measured on a degree-8 design over eleven points, the condition estimate falls 3.32×10¹⁰ → 1.07×10⁸ → 2.71×10⁶ → 4.57×10⁴ → 778 → 10.1 across six values of λ. Unregularised, ten of a double's sixteen digits are already gone — see condition-number-as-digits-lost. The cost side is asserted too: on data a plain fit handles well, ridge makes the training error strictly worse. That is what the parameter is for.

    Training error cannot choose a model. It is monotone in the degree by construction, so selecting on it picks the largest candidate whatever the data. Validation error is not monotone, and that non-monotonicity is what pays for the split — held-out-error-over-training-error.

    The two classifiers are opposite ends of one trade. κ-NN computes zero distances at fit time and exactly len(train) per query; naive Bayes counts once and predicts in features × classes regardless of training-set size. Both counted by the implementations rather than described — lazy-versus-eager-learners, and counted-complexity-assertions again.

    Where the arithmetic breaks. The naive Bayes log-posterior reaches −115.8 at a 400-word vocabulary, −259.6 at 900, −461.0 at 1,600, and −748.7 at 2,600 — the first that exponentiates to exactly 0.0. See log-domain-arithmetic-for-probability-products.

    #Three claims the tests corrected

    A fifty-fold convergence claim measured twelve-fold: 76 gradient iterations against 6 for IRLS. The measurement was right and the claim was not, so the claim changed — and what the numbers show is better than a ratio. Gradient ascent takes 677, 149 or 76 iterations depending on a step size that nothing in the data suggests; IRLS takes 6 with no such parameter at all.

    κ-NN's zero-training-error guarantee has a precondition nobody states: it holds only while no two identical feature vectors disagree on a label. Found by writing the test wrong — it added a contradictory duplicate, expected 1.0, and measured 0.8. Real text data is full of duplicate feature vectors, so the precondition now has its own test.

    Accuracy hides the failure that matters. An all-negative classifier scores 0.90 when positives are one in ten, which is exactly the shape of the course's face-detection task. The empty column in the confusion matrix is where it shows.

    All three follow document-the-failure-mode-next-to-the-fix and are the reason this page claims tests-that-pass-for-the-wrong-reason is the failure mode worth designing against.

    #Provenance, and a correction

    The laboratory specifications are the work of A. Gonczarek, J.M. Tomczak, S. Zaręba, M. Zięba, J. Kaczmar and P. Klukowski. The code skeletons are headed Authors: A. Gonczarek, J. Kaczmar, S. Zareba — DO NOT MODIFY THIS FILE, and in the archive copy content.py is still the unfilled stub. The archive's own completed solutions sit separately, in 6th/SADiSM/Sadism/1..3.

    None of the three was copied. A solution written inside someone else's scaffold cannot be lifted out of it cleanly, so the methods are re-derived here in a structure of this repository's own — provenance-notice-file.

    This repository is a correction. An earlier coverage matrix excluded the whole 6th/SADiSM folder because Bishop and Murphy sit in it. Excluding the textbooks was right; the four laboratory specifications and three solution trees beside them were excluded alongside them without ever being named. The full accounting is in pwr-subject-notes.

    #2026-08-12 reference audit

    The dependency-free Python suite passed 75/75 tests. The audit preserved the existing visual guide because its stated regularisation, validation, classifier-cost, and numerical stability examples are the repository's measured teaching artefacts; the uncommitted README and SVG changes pre-dated this pass. The checked local branch is main.

    #Status

    Complete and committed locally on main. No remote. List 4 — an open-ended free-choice classifier — is deliberately not rebuilt: the task has no fixed specification, so there is nothing to assert.
