    Heuristic Over Exact Max-Cut

    Decision. Solve Max-Cut with best-improvement local search plus ten random restarts, instead of searching the partition space exhaustively.

    Date. 2026-03-20, the first lab committed to discrete-mathematics-laboratories.

    #Why

    The rejected alternative is quantified in the lab itself: with 127 vertices the space of partitions is 2^127. That number is not a rhetorical flourish — it is the argument, and the lab reports it rather than leaving "intractable" as an adjective.

    "The thread running through all six: take a formal object (a cut, a modulus, a set, a recursion, a graph, an information system) and make its behaviour observable."

    Discrete-Mathematics-Laboratories/README.md, 2026-07-10

    Stating the size of the space you are not searching is what makes the heuristic legible as a choice — see make-the-formal-object-observable and max-cut-and-local-search.

    #Alternatives rejected

    Option Why not
    Exhaustive enumeration2^127 partitions
    Exact solver (ILP / branch-and-bound)a dependency and a modelling layer, for a lab about the algorithm
    Goemans–Williamson SDP0.878-approximation with a proof, but needs an SDP solver — no dependency in this repo is heavier than matplotlib
    Single-start local searchone descent lands wherever its start drains to; the restarts are the whole improvement

    #What was given up

    An approximation guarantee. Multi-start local search has no proven ratio — the result is "the best of ten local optima", which could be far from optimal on an adversarial instance and there is no bound saying otherwise. The lab has no comparison against a known optimum or a published bound, so solution quality is unmeasured. TODO: confidence: low on any effectiveness claim.

    This is the same species of trade as the one on shortest-path-algorithm-family, where a function named tspChristofides gives up the 1.5-approximation guarantee by matching greedily — with the difference that here the loss of guarantee is deliberate and there it is unremarked.
